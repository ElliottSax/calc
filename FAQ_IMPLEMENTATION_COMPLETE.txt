SCHEMA STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Format: YAML frontmatter in MDX files
Type: schema.org FAQPage v1.0
Structure:
  - @context: https://schema.org
  - @type: FAQPage
  - mainEntity: [Array of Question/Answer pairs]
    - @type: Question
    - name: [Question text]
    - acceptedAnswer:
        - @type: Answer
        - text: [Answer text]
